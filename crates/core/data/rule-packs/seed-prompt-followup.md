# Rule pack seed prompt: follow-up on the hospital's outpatient side

Sent to each model after its first proposals (`seed-prompt.md`) were written, because the first round left a gap: the brief asked
for waiting, exam and support relationships for hospitals, and the hospital proposals held almost none.

Propose 3 to 5 more rules for the `hospital` archetype only, about its outpatient or clinic side: waiting areas, exam rooms,
nurse and support spaces, clean and dirty (soiled) supply, patient privacy. Same rule shape, same rules of the road as the first
prompt: original prose, honest confidence, real exceptions, no standards text.

Append them to the end of the `hospital` list in your own proposals file, and change nothing else in it. Use keys that do not
repeat one already in the list.
